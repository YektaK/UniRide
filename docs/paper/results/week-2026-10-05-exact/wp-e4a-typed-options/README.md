# WP-E4a: exact typed options against the WP-E10 GA options (week of 2026-10-05)

Design: [`EXACT_SOLVER_AMPL_DESIGN.md`](../../../../designs/EXACT_SOLVER_AMPL_DESIGN.md) sections 1.2, 1.5, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 (row WP-E4); decisions DECISION_LOG E01-E05 and H01-H06. Protocol `exact_reference_v1`: these exact rows are never pooled, ranked or tested together with heuristic rows. This is a descriptive archive of one snapshot week. The GA side is one seed-42 run under the native (secondary) termination protocol, taken only from the WP-E10 re-runs (`week-2026-10-05-fleet-rerun-large-sedan`, `week-2026-10-05-fleet-rerun-doblo-cap3`, pin `f29aa3d88488`, clean tree). No superiority claim is made, in either direction. A gap is the difference between that one GA run and a proven exact value or bound; it is not a property of the GA in general.

Not part of WP-E4a (WP-E4b, later): the union-menu day selection, the TypeScript confirmation CLI `scripts/verify-typed-selection.ts` and `day_gaps.csv`.

| `summary.json` | per (fleet, R, option kind) counts: statuses, comparison labels, `dK` zero and positive, `gD`, exact <= GA, HiGHS agreement, solver seconds |

`option_gaps.csv`: `option` is `base` (single-type SP on the fixed large type, compared with the GA `base`) or `q0..qQ` (typed SP with at most `q` large routes, minimising the routes of the minimised type, compared with the GA option `qN` of the same kind). `k_kind` is `K` for `base` and `K_m` (routes of the minimised type: sedan, or minivan) for `q` rows; `K_GA`, `K_LB`, `K_star` and `dK` follow that kind (so `dK` of a `q` row is `dK_m`). `K_LB` of a `q` row is the stage-1 dual bound only (design 1.2); for `base` it is the maximum of the solver bound, the capacity floors and the clique floor. `D_*` is the sum of tour minutes in integer minutes; `D_LB` is conditional on `K = K*`, `D_LB_free` has no count objective; `gD` is defined only when `K_GA = K*`, otherwise `gD_note = not_comparable_K_GA_gt_Kstar` and only `gD_free` is shown. `comparison` labels the feasibility relation of the two options: `compared`, `both_infeasible`, `ga_infeasible_exact_feasible`, `exact_infeasible_ga_feasible` (blocking), `exact_infeasible_ga_unavailable`, `ga_option_unavailable`, `exact_unsolved`. GA labels: `ga_protocol` native, `ga_seed` 42, `ga_commit` `f29aa3d`, `ga_dirty_tree` false (from the source manifests). `ga_cert_archived` is the certificate archived by WP-E10, `ga_recertified` the certificate of this run (same `certify_optimization_response` call as for the exact routes, typed path, on the archived matrix slice), `ga_cert_agree` whether the two agree.

## What was run

- Code commit `de549d3b112e` (clean tree; the manifest `dirty_working_tree` is false), branch `feat/wp-e4a-typed-options`. Python: `.venv-jit` with `PYTHONPATH` set to the worktree root; the manifest `python_provenance` shows the three packages imported from this checkout. Gurobi 13.0.3 (primary), HiGHS 1.15.1 (verification), AMPL 20260809, amplpy 0.18.0, threads 1, seed 0, 60 s / 20 work units per solver call and 600 s / 200 on the peak waves (peak = the largest wave of each service date, D-B; 180 of the 1872 rows). CP-SAT 9.15.6755 for the replay.
- Command: `scripts/run_exact_typed_menus.py --jobs 4 --unit-timeout 5400` (one worker process per (fleet, R, date) unit; results merged in a fixed order; per-unit cache). Four parallel workers shared the machine, which can only change the wall time of the time-limited HiGHS calls; none stopped on a limit.
- Run history, stated because it affects what the manifest wall time means: a first attempt with 10 worker processes and a second with 4, both in a process pool, each ended with workers waiting for an AMPL call that never returned; I killed them and they wrote no results. The campaign was then run with per-unit worker processes. In that run two workers (large+doblo_cap3 R = 90, dates 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-09) waited again on a stalled AMPL call; I killed them and the runner restarted those two units from scratch (the rule: a unit that is killed or exceeds the wall limit is retried once). Two HiGHS stage-1 calls failed with solver failures (`solve_result_num` 999 in `large+sedan` 2026-10-08 R90 `pickup:705` q1 and 500 in `large+doblo_cap3` 2026-10-05 R70 `pickup:525` q1; Gurobi had proven both options optimal), which the runner reported as two blocking errors. Only those two (fleet, R, date) units were re-solved from scratch at the same commit; the other 38 units come from the same run through the per-unit cache. All values in these files are therefore from the one commit and one solver configuration; the manifest `total_wall_seconds` (292 s) is the wall time of that last invocation (replay and two units) only. The solver seconds per row in `option_gaps.csv` are those of the solve that produced the row.
- Part 1 gate (replay acceptance, CP-SAT, no AMPL): the Python port rebuilt the `/internal/fleet-selection` request of every (date, R, L) and the archived request is equal field by field; the solver call with the router's effective parameters reproduced the archived response (`status`, `cars`, `large_peak`, `car_minutes`, `total_minutes`, `selection` with option ids, indices and labels) in all 120 cells: 60 of 60 for large+doblo_cap3 (all `optimal`) and 60 of 60 for large+sedan (31 `optimal`, 29 `infeasible_for_L`, the proven infeasibility being reproduced exactly; label `match_infeasible_for_L`). CP-SAT seed (20261008) and worker count (1) equal the archive in all cells. Evidence: `replay.csv`.

## Result (descriptive; one seed-42 GA run)

1872 options: large+sedan 1004 (228 `base`, 776 `q`), large+doblo_cap3 868 (228 `base`, 640 `q`). Every enumeration completed with model `SP` (no count cap fired; at most 47 027 columns). Per option all three Gurobi stages stopped by `optimal` (1705 options) or the model was proven infeasible (167 `q` options, all large+sedan, `stop_reason infeasible`); no option is `feasible_with_gap`, `bound_only`, `rejected` or `timing_dependent`, none is `non_lexicographic`.

| Check | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Exact certificate (typed path through `certify_optimization_response`) | `is_feasible: true` for all 1705 solved options; the objective cross-check in integer arithmetic matches for all 1705 |
| GA re-certificate vs the archived WP-E10 certificate | agree in all 1703 options that have GA routes (`ga_cert_agree` true; both feasible); the other 169 GA options have no routes (`skipped_infeasible`) |
| Exact <= GA (`K` or `K_m` first, then `D`) | holds in all 1703 options where both sides are feasible (`exact_le_ga` true); no violation, no `K_GA < K_LB` |
| Exact infeasible with a feasible GA option | 0 (it would be a blocking model/semantics mismatch) |
| GA infeasible with a feasible exact option | 2 rows, labelled `ga_infeasible_exact_feasible`: large+sedan 2026-10-05 `pickup:525` (20 legs, the peak wave) option `q2` at R = 50 (`K_m* = 4`, `D* = 463`) and at R = 60 (`K_m* = 3`, `D* = 395`). The GA answered `skipped_infeasible` there. Not an error by itself (the exact option is certified feasible); reported as found |
| Both infeasible (`both_infeasible`) | 167 `q` options, all large+sedan (46, 41, 40, 40 at R = 50, 60, 70, 90): the exact model proves the option infeasible and the GA answered `skipped_infeasible` |
| HiGHS re-solve of every Gurobi-optimal or infeasible-proven option (stage 1, stage 2, free) | `highs_agrees` true in all 1872 options: HiGHS proved the same `(K*, D*)` (1705 options) or the same infeasibility (167 options); no disagreement, no HiGHS result left unproven |

`dK` (`K` for `base`, `K_m` for `q`): `dK = 0` in 56 of 57 `base` options per (fleet, R) and in 144-159 of 148-160 solved `q` options per (fleet, R); the options with `dK = 1` are 4 `base` and 10 `q` per fleet (maximum `dK` is 1 everywhere). `gD > 0` where defined: 15 `base` options per fleet, 34 (large+sedan) and 39 (large+doblo_cap3) `q` options; the largest `gD` of a `base` option is 0.089947 (R = 60) and of a `q` option 0.131737 (large+sedan, R = 90); the mean `gD` over all comparable `q` options is 0.00159. Per (fleet, R, kind) counts and maxima are in `summary.json`; the `base` rows at the shared fixed type reproduce the single-type WP-E3 picture (4 `base` options with `dK = 1` per fleet, one 08:45 pickup wave per R).

Solver time: Gurobi solve calls 3 844 s and HiGHS re-solves 9 108 s summed over all rows (largest single option: Gurobi 15.8 s, HiGHS 621 s in total over its three stages on the 20-leg peak wave).

## Limits of these results

- One snapshot week (5 service days, 57 waves per R), one GA seed (42), one native-termination run. GA gaps here describe that run only.
- The per-option comparison is per wave and per option kind. It does not say anything about the day-level selection; the union-menu day selection is WP-E4b.
- `ga_infeasible_exact_feasible` rows are a statement about one GA answer (no feasible typed split found), not about GA behaviour in general.
- The certificate has no waiting time (design section 3 note), shared by GA, model and certificate.
